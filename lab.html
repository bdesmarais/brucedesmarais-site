Prospective students

I advise Ph.D. students in Political Science and the dual-title program in Social Data Analytics at Penn State. If you are interested in policy diffusion, networks, AI, or digital politics, apply to one of these programs and mention my lab in your statement. You are welcome to email me with a short description of your interests.
