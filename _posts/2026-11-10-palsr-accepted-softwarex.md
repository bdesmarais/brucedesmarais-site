---
title: "palsr accepted at SoftwareX"
tags: [publication, software]
---
Our paper on **palsr**, an R package for modeling dyadic interactions between moving actors with Projected Actor Locations, has been accepted for publication in *SoftwareX*. The paper is coauthored with Sangyeon Kim and Howard Liu, both alumni of the lab. The package is available on [CRAN](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=palsr).
